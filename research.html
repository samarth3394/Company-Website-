CEREON AI

An AI-Based Operating System Concept

Vision: Building a more intelligent, automated, and human-centric computing experience through Artificial Intelligence.

CEREON AI is an innovative AI-Based Operating System concept focused on integrating Artificial Intelligence directly into the computing experience. The vision behind CEREON AI is to move beyond traditional operating systems, where users primarily interact with applications and manual commands, toward an intelligent environment where AI can understand user intent, plan tasks, and assist with system-level operations.

CEREON AI is currently being researched and developed under Bharti Nexus Technologies as part of an ongoing Research and Development (R&D) initiative. The research focuses on exploring the integration of Artificial Intelligence, automation, computer control, intelligent task execution, and human-computer interaction into a unified computing environment.

The AI Orchestrator

The core of CEREON AI is an AI Orchestrator, which acts as the central intelligence layer. It interprets user instructions, understands context, plans tasks, selects the appropriate agents or tools, and coordinates their execution.

Proposed Intelligent Architecture

A modular, agentic approach to system orchestration.

🦈

AI Orchestrator

Handles intent understanding, planning, reasoning, task decomposition, and agent coordination.

🌐

Browser Agent

Performs web-related tasks through browsers such as Chrome and Edge autonomously.

💻

Computer Agent

Interacts natively with applications, window management, and computer controls.

📁

File Agent

Searches, organizes, analyzes, and manages files, folders, and documents intelligently.

⚡

Tool Execution Layer

Executes tasks through Python, PowerShell, APIs, and operating-system commands.

💾

Memory & Context System

Maintains relevant task context and information to provide continuous, intelligent interactions.

🔮

Security & Permission Layer

Controls system-level operations ensuring that sensitive actions require appropriate authorization.

The Long-Term Vision

The long-term vision of CEREON AI is to explore how an operating system can evolve from being primarily a platform for running applications into an intelligent computing environment that actively assists users with their work.

The ongoing research may explore areas such as AI agents, Large Language Models (LLMs), local and cloud-based AI, intelligent memory, computer automation, system-level AI interaction, privacy, security, and AI-native user interfaces.

Through this research, Bharti Nexus Technologies aims to explore the possibilities of creating a more intelligent, automated, secure, and human-centric computing experience powered by Artificial Intelligence.
